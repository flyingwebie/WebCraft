# WebCraft Contributor Licence Agreement

> **Draft.** This agreement has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. It will be reviewed, and may change, before WebCraft's first public release ([ADR 0012](docs/adr/0012-agpl-core-with-module-and-site-exception.md)). Contributions accepted under this draft will be covered by the reviewed version only with the contributor's agreement.

This agreement is between you (the **Contributor**) and Flying Web Solutions, the maintainer of the WebCraft project (the **Maintainer**). It covers every Contribution you submit to WebCraft.

## 1. Definitions

- **Contribution**: any work of authorship, such as code, documentation, designs or tests, that you intentionally submit to the WebCraft project, for example in a pull request, a patch or an issue, for inclusion in WebCraft.
- **Submit**: any form of communication sent to the Maintainer or to the project's repositories, issue tracker or other channels, except communication you clearly mark as "Not a Contribution".

## 5. What you confirm

You confirm that:

1. each Contribution is your original work, or you have the right to submit it under this agreement;
2. if your employer or anyone else has rights in your Contributions, you have their permission to make them under this agreement, or they have waived those rights;
3. you will tell the Maintainer if any of these statements stops being true;
4. you will clearly identify, when you submit it, any part of a Contribution that is not your original work, along with its source and licence.

## 6. No warranty and no obligation

You provide your Contributions "as is", without warranties or conditions of any kind. The Maintainer is not obliged to use any Contribution.

## 7. How to agree

Include this line in the description of your first pull request:

> I have read the WebCraft Contributor Licence Agreement (CLA.md) and agree to it for this and all my future contributions to WebCraft.

If you contribute on behalf of an organisation, say which one, and confirm you are authorised to agree for it.
